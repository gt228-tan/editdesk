EditDesk - Client Portal by GT Edits

It is a client portal designed for video editors, freelance creatives, and creative agencies which also  simplify communication, project tracking, deliverable approvals, and payment management.

Key Features:
AI Brief Planner and Cost Estimation:
Our AI Brief Planner uses Google Gemini API to turn client requirements into a clear project brief. It finds missing details, asks questions, suggests deadlines, and recommends suitable file formats.
Our AI estimates project costs by identifying keywords in client requests and matching them with a predefined rate chart.
It reduces manual work by helping admins prepare project briefs, estimate costs, and plan project timelines faster.
It helps clients share their requirements clearly and understand the expected cost, timeline, and deliverables, making the overall process easier and smoother.

Google Drive Automation :
Integrates with Google Drive API v3.
Automatically creates dedicated client folders.
Also has quick deliverable for admin panel.
Reduces manual file organization and repetitive setup work.

Email Updates : 
Admin: View and manage new work requests.
Admin: Approve, request revisions, or reject deliverables.
Client: Approve or reject work requests and upload deliverables.
Client: Receive payment reminders and track pending payments.
