Workflow

Key Features

A frictionless, automated four-step journey from raw client concept to paid, finalized deliverables.

01

Voice Briefing & Gemini AI

Clients dictate or type project requirements using browser speech-to-text. Google Gemini AI parses prompts, identifies missing specs, suggests turnaround times, and calculates cost estimates against your rate chart.

Voice & AI Scoping
02

Automated Drive Workspaces

Upon brief approval, EditDesk triggers Google Drive API v3 to automatically spin up client directory trees, private upload targets for raw footage, and a quick-deliverable admin panel to cut out manual setup.

Zero Manual Folders
03

Review, Revise & Calendly

Clients stream draft cuts in-portal, check watermarking indicators, and submit itemized timestamped revision checklists. Integrated 1-click Calendly allows instant meeting scheduling for project sync calls.

Timecode Notes & Sync
04

Invoicing, PDF & Brevo Sync

Admins generate branded invoices with sequential numbering (#001), advance payment deductions, and digital signatures. Brevo API dispatches automated payment reminders with the generated PDF attached.

Vector PDF & Email Sync
